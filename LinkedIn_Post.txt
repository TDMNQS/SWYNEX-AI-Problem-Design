Completed the problem-design document for Task 1 of my internship with @SWYNEX Technologies.

My chosen use case is evidence-based AI question answering over five research papers about retrieval and language models. The design aims to help students locate answers, inspect supporting passages, and identify questions the selected papers cannot answer.

I documented the target user, data sources, scope, proposed RAG workflow, constraints, and evaluation plan. The proposed evaluation covers retrieval quality, answer correctness, citation support, abstention, latency, and cost.

My main takeaway: a useful AI problem needs clear boundaries and measurable success criteria before implementation begins.

This submission covers the design stage; implementation and benchmark results are future work.
